CC0 places the work in the public domain: free to use in personal, educational
and commercial projects, with no restriction and no attribution required.
Kenney asks for credit voluntarily, so this file exists. The credit also
appears on the about screen.

These PNGs are not the pack's own preview images, which are 64px thumbnails and
too small to use. They are baked from the pack's .glb models with three.js, lit
from the upper left to match LIGHT in src/components/kid/art.tsx, trimmed to
their alpha bounds, scaled so every object has the same visual weight, and
quantised to a 256 colour palette. The bake script is not committed: it is a
one-off, and the inputs are a public download.

  256x256, RGBA, ~5 KB each.
