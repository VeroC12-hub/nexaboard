Drop a background music loop here as `theme.mp3`.

The game plays it on a loop from the first tap and ducks it to a fifth of its
volume whenever anybody speaks. If the file is missing there is simply no
music: `music.onerror` clears it and nothing else changes.

What it needs to be:

  Gentle, warm and loopable, under a minute is fine. Kalimba, marimba or soft
  hand percussion suits Ananse and suits the setting.

  Seamless at the loop point. A bump every thirty seconds is worse than
  silence once a child has heard it twenty times.

  Quiet and uneventful. It plays under a spoken instruction, so anything with
  a tune in the same register as a voice competes with the thing the child
  actually has to hear.
